# Little Snowy Owl Studio — Phonics Pond — Brand guidelines for AI outputs

Offline, calm, child-friendly phonics learning app from Little Snowy Owl Studio.

## Must
- Words must be decodable at the child's current level
- Tricky words only after they are introduced
- Consistent characters across storybooks
- Human review of every AI-assisted asset

## Never
- AI chat or live AI features for children
- Network-dependent features
- Collecting children's data

## Voice
Clear, warm, slow narration; simple sentences.

## Languages
English.

## How these rules are enforced
- Encoded as machine-checkable rules where possible (see Week 1 `brand_assistant/brands/*.json`
  for Balena, CheckKhum and Little Snowy Owl).
- Visual rules are scored with a rubric in `EVALUATION.md`.
- A human approves anything published.
