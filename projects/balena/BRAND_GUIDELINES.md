# Balena Roastery — Brand guidelines for AI outputs

Balena is a short, round whale mascot for a Thai coffee roastery.

## Must
- Short, round whale body
- Coffee cup
- Water splash
- White striped belly
- Pink cheeks
- Product packaging must match approved product references exactly

## Never
- Legs or feet (Balena must **never** have legs)
- Invented packaging text, logos or colours
- Health claims about coffee

## Voice
Warm, friendly, playful Thai; short sentences; practical brewing tips.

## Languages
Thai (primary), English (secondary).

## How these rules are enforced
- Encoded as machine-checkable rules where possible (see Week 1 `brand_assistant/brands/*.json`
  for Balena, CheckKhum and Little Snowy Owl).
- Visual rules are scored with a rubric in `EVALUATION.md`.
- A human approves anything published.
