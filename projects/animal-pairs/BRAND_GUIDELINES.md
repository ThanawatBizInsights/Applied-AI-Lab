# Little Snowy Owl Studio — Animal Pairs — Brand guidelines for AI outputs

Soft, gentle, calm animal friends for young children; lavender cards; Little Snowy Owl Studio's mascot is Snowy.

## Must
- Child-friendly, calm visuals
- Consistent character identity across poses
- Transparent, game-ready PNG assets
- Accessibility: VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion

## Never
- Ads, dark patterns or manipulative purchase prompts
- Data collection from children
- AI features that interact with children
- Timers or failure pressure

## Voice
Gentle, reassuring, parent-facing for marketing; simple and kind in-game.

## Languages
English (primary).

## How these rules are enforced
- Encoded as machine-checkable rules where possible (see Week 1 `brand_assistant/brands/*.json`
  for Balena, CheckKhum and Little Snowy Owl).
- Visual rules are scored with a rubric in `EVALUATION.md`.
- A human approves anything published.
