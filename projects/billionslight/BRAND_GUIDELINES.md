# Billionslight — Brand guidelines for AI outputs

Thai solar and electrical products business with educational content; mascot **Sunny**.

## Must
- Technically accurate electrical content (validated against the encoded rules)
- Consistent Sunny mascot
- Clear safety warnings for electrical work

## Never
- Unsafe DIY electrical instructions
- Specs or prices not taken from a cited source
- Guaranteed savings claims

## Voice
Friendly, expert, practical Thai; explains technical terms simply.

## Languages
Thai (primary).

## How these rules are enforced
- Encoded as machine-checkable rules where possible (see Week 1 `brand_assistant/brands/*.json`
  for Balena, CheckKhum and Little Snowy Owl).
- Visual rules are scored with a rubric in `EVALUATION.md`.
- A human approves anything published.
