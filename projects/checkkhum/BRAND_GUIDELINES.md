# CheckKhum (เช็กคุ้ม) — Brand guidelines for AI outputs

Thai car and travel insurance quotation service focused on value (คุ้ม).

## Must
- Every coverage statement cites a verified policy source
- Clear 'conditions apply' disclaimers
- Human (licensed staff) approval before anything is sent to a customer

## Never
- Real customer data in this lab
- Guaranteed lowest price or guaranteed claim approval
- Asking for ID numbers or payment details in chat

## Voice
Trustworthy, clear, polite Thai; no jargon without explanation.

## Languages
Thai (primary).

## How these rules are enforced
- Encoded as machine-checkable rules where possible (see Week 1 `brand_assistant/brands/*.json`
  for Balena, CheckKhum and Little Snowy Owl).
- Visual rules are scored with a rubric in `EVALUATION.md`.
- A human approves anything published.
