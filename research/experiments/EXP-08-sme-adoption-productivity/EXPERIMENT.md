# EXP-08 — AI adoption and productivity improvements in SMEs

**Status:** DESIGN (not started) · **Weeks:** 13–24 (longitudinal) · **Projects:** [Balena Roastery](../../../projects/balena/README.md), [CheckKhum (เช็กคุ้ม)](../../../projects/checkkhum/README.md), [Billionslight](../../../projects/billionslight/README.md), [CheePao](../../../projects/cheepao/README.md)

## 1. Research question
Which AI-enabled workflows produce measurable productivity gains in the learner's small businesses, and which adoption factors (usefulness, ease of use, trust) explain sustained use?

## 2. Hypothesis
H1: Workflows with high perceived usefulness and a human approval step show sustained use after 8 weeks and measurable time savings; low-trust workflows are abandoned. H0: no relationship.
