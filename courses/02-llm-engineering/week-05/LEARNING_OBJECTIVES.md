# Week 05 — Learning objectives

By the end of this week you should be able to:

1. Explain embeddings geometrically: dot product, cosine similarity, normalisation.
2. Compare exact and approximate nearest-neighbour search (IVF, HNSW) and their trade-offs.
3. Store and query embeddings in Postgres with pgvector, with suitable indexes.
4. Evaluate retrieval with recall@k and MRR on a labelled query set.
5. Choose an embedding model for Thai and English text with evidence.

## Competencies affected (see `PROGRESS.md`)
Evidence from this week can raise competencies by **at most one level**, and only with a linked
commit, test or report.
