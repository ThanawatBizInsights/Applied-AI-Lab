# Week 21 — Learning objectives

By the end of this week you should be able to:

1. Expose the Gateway and Knowledge Engine as a typed FastAPI service.
2. Containerise the service with a small, reproducible image.
3. Add authentication (API keys or JWT) and per-project authorisation.
4. Deploy to a staging environment (free tier) with human approval.

## Competencies affected (see `PROGRESS.md`)
Evidence from this week can raise competencies by **at most one level**, and only with a linked
commit, test or report.
