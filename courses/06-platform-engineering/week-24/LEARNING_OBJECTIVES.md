# Week 24 — Learning objectives

By the end of this week you should be able to:

1. Write portfolio case studies with problem, architecture, evidence and limitations.
2. Present a live capstone demonstration with a fallback recording.
3. Complete EXP-08 (SME AI adoption and productivity) as a short research paper.
4. Assess your own competency levels with evidence and plan the next 6 months.

## Competencies affected (see `PROGRESS.md`)
Evidence from this week can raise competencies by **at most one level**, and only with a linked
commit, test or report.
