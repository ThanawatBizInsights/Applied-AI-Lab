# Week 09 — Learning objectives

By the end of this week you should be able to:

1. Explain forward noising, reverse denoising and the noise-prediction objective.
2. Explain why latent diffusion uses a VAE and what the text encoder does.
3. Explain samplers, steps, CFG scale and seeds, and predict their effect.
4. Run ComfyUI on a cloud GPU and build a text-to-image graph from empty.
5. Record a reproducible generation (model hash, seed, sampler, workflow JSON).

## Competencies affected (see `PROGRESS.md`)
Evidence from this week can raise competencies by **at most one level**, and only with a linked
commit, test or report.
