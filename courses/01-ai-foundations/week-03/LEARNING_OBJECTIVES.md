# Week 03 — Learning objectives

By the end of this week you should be able to:

1. Explain why prompts work: conditioning, in-context learning and instruction tuning.
2. Design system prompts with role, constraints, examples and an output contract.
3. Produce schema-validated JSON outputs and handle validation failures with retries.
4. Send image inputs (product photo, packaging) and evaluate the answers.
5. Version prompts and compare variants with a small labelled set.

## Competencies affected (see `PROGRESS.md`)
Evidence from this week can raise competencies by **at most one level**, and only with a linked
commit, test or report.
