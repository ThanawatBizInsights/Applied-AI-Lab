# Week 17 — Learning objectives

By the end of this week you should be able to:

1. Explain scaling laws, post-training (instruction tuning, RLHF) and reasoning models.
2. Explain test-time compute and when extended reasoning is worth the cost.
3. Evaluate multimodal models on image, document and audio understanding tasks.
4. Design EXP-05 (phonics curriculum compliance).
